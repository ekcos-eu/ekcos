Why an ordinary screen stays for centuries
EVA is a durable material. Without a biodegradation additive, a used screen can remain in landfill for an
estimated 300 to 1,000 years. Foam clogs and flip-flops are made from the same material, and they do not
biodegrade either.

Estimated time to break down in landfill.

Our solution: the Eco-One™ additive
During manufacturing, we add about 1% Eco-One™ to the material. It does not affect the product’s
strength, appearance or performance.
In a biologically active landfill, Eco-One™ allows the material to break down into biogas, carbon dioxide and
humus. Modern landfills capture the biogas and use it for energy.

Useful during its service life, and after it.
Instead of lying in landfill for centuries, the material breaks down into biogas that modern
landfills use for energy, and into humus. Humus, in turn, makes soil richer.

Independent test: 93% vs. 0%
In an independent test under ASTM D5511 and ISO 15985 at the Instituto Politécnico Nacional in Mexico City,
ëkcos EVA with Eco-One™ reached 93% biodegradation within 3.5 years, while the same material without
the additive showed 0%. Up to 99% biodegradation is projected within 5 years.
