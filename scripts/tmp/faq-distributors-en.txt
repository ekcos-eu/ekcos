        Materials, Eco-One™ and packaging

     What are ëkcos products      Our urinal screens, toilet bowl clips and air freshener inserts are made from a
     made of?                     proprietary material infused with a deodorant-based fragrance. The material is
                                  made with recycled EVA. The share of recycled EVA varies from batch to batch,
                                  so we do not state a fixed percentage. Every ëkcos product except ëkco mat
                                  contains the Eco-One™ additive.


     What is Eco-One™?            Eco-One™ is an organic additive made by EcoLogic, LLC. We add about 1% of it
                                  to our material during production. It does not change the product’s strength,
                                  appearance or performance. When the product ends up in a biologically active
                                  landfill, the material breaks down into biogas, CO₂ and humus. Modern landfills
                                  use the biogas for energy.


     What does “biodegradable     It means that our material breaks down in a biologically active landfill, where
     in landfill” mean, and how   used screens end up. The Instituto Politécnico Nacional in Mexico City tested
     was it tested?               ëkcos EVA with Eco-One™ under the standards ASTM D5511 and ISO 15985. It
                                  reached 93% biodegradation within 3.5 years, while the same material without
                                  the additive showed 0%. Up to 99% is projected within 5 years. This is a
                                  projection from the measured trend, not a measured result. Biodegradation
                                  depends on landfill conditions.




     Why don’t you call your      Since 27 September 2026, EU rules (Directive (EU) 2024/825) no longer allow
     products “eco-friendly”?     general claims such as “eco-friendly”, “green” or “sustainable” without
                                  recognized proof of excellent environmental performance. So we tell you
                                  exactly what we can prove: made with recycled EVA, biodegradable in landfill
                                  with Eco-One™ in an independent laboratory test, and boxes made from 100%
                                  recycled cardboard.


     Can ëkcos products be        The EVA material itself can be recycled. A used urinal screen or toilet bowl clip
     recycled?                    cannot, because it is biologically contaminated, so it belongs in general waste.
                                  The inserts of frësh drop and ëkco loop are made with recycled EVA and are not
                                  contaminated, so their material can be recycled. Please follow your local waste
                                  rules. We do not call our products “100% recyclable”, because that would not be
                                  true for a used screen.


     What packaging do you        Our product boxes and master cases are made from 100% recycled corrugated
     use?                         cardboard. The product bags are made of BOPP, a polypropylene film. We make
                                  no environmental claim about the bags. Packaging documentation under the
